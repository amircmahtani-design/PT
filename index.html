App and storage
Recover lost data
The app keeps three copies on this device: the main store, a backup copy, and an IndexedDB mirror. If your history vanished, check here first — one of them often survives.
Cloud sync
Deleting the app from your phone deletes its local storage with it. Cloud sync is the only thing that survives that. Set it up, then tap Check my cloud backup — if that doesn't say "Backup found", you are not backed up.
Your training history lives on this device (localStorage + IndexedDB) and survives redeploys — a new index.html on the same domain never wipes it. Turn this on and every lift is also mirrored to your own Firebase project, so it survives a lost phone, a cleared browser, and syncs across devices.
