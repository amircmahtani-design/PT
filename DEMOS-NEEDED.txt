DEMO IMAGES STILL NEEDED — v119 · 15/09/2026
============================================

Every movement the app can put in front of you with NO picture or the WRONG
one, in priority order. Tier 1 is the one that matters: those are on screen
every single session.

HOW TO HAND THEM OVER
---------------------
Either form works, animated is better:

  demos/<name>.gif                an animated clip, 2-4 seconds, looping
  demos/<name>-1.jpg and -2.jpg   or two stills: START position, END position

  · square-ish; 400-600px on the short side is plenty
  · GIFs under ~1.5MB each, stills under ~200KB — they get cached on the phone
  · keep the exact file name listed against each movement and the wiring is
    mechanical on my side
  · put them in a demos/ folder in the repo, or send me URLs

EASIEST ACCURATE ROUTE: film yourself. Ten seconds per movement, phone
propped side-on. Tier 1 is 17 clips, about twenty minutes of work, perfectly
accurate, and no question about whose images they are — which stock photos
and YouTube stills on a published app very much are.

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN THEY ARRIVE
=============================
The demos/ folder goes into the service worker so they cache on the phone
and work offline, a local-first lookup goes in ahead of every other source,
and the emoji fallback stays for whatever is still missing. Nothing else
changes, and nothing already working gets touched.
