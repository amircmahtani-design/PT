CHILD'S POSE — SHOT, AND IN (v134)
==================================
demos/child-s-pose-1.jpg and -2.jpg, 720×480, both frames from one photo
Amir supplied: hands and knees, then folded with the hips down on the heels.

The free pack's own "Child's Pose" record was matching at 1.31 — the highest
score the matcher produces — and its written instructions were right ("sit on
your heels") while both of its photographs showed the hips high above the
heels. A name matcher cannot look at a picture, so nothing could have caught
it. That record stays named in FED_WRONG: a local file wins anyway, and if one
ever fails to load the fallback must be the "Watch how" link, not back to it.

One file covers both movements — "Child's pose" by its own slug, and "Child's
pose with side reach" through the pack name it points at.


WHERE THEY LIVE
===============
demos/<movement>-1.jpg and -2.jpg, next to index.html. The app builds the
filename from the movement's own name, so adding another one later is just a
matter of dropping the two files in with the right name and adding the slug to
the LOCAL_DEMOS list in index.html and the DEMOS list in sw.js.

The service worker pre-caches all 150 on install, one at a time so a single
missing file cannot take the whole offline app down with it. They work in a
basement gym with no signal.
