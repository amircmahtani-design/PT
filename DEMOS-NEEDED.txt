ONE IS NEEDED: CHILD'S POSE
===========================
demos/child-s-pose-1.jpg and demos/child-s-pose-2.jpg

The free pack has a record called "Child's Pose" and the app was matching it
with total confidence — a score of 1.31, the highest the matcher produces.
The record's written instructions are correct: "lower your buttocks down to
sit on your heels". Its two photographs are not. In both frames the hips stay
high above the heels and the thighs are near vertical, which is a kneeling
tuck. Child's pose is defined by the hips resting ON the heels.

The matcher reads names. It cannot look at a picture, so nothing in it could
ever have caught this. The record is now named in FED_WRONG and never used,
and both movements that pointed at it — "Child's pose" and "Child's pose with
side reach" — fall back to the "Watch how" link until there is a photograph.

  START   kneeling, hips down on the heels, back tall, arms resting
  END     folded forward, hips still ON the heels, forehead to the mat,
          arms long in front

Drop the two files in, add "child-s-pose" to LOCAL_DEMOS in index.html and to
DEMOS in sw.js, and it takes over on its own — a local photograph outranks
every remote source. One file covers both movements.

WHERE THEY LIVE
===============
demos/<movement>-1.jpg and -2.jpg, next to index.html. The app builds the
filename from the movement's own name, so adding another one later is just a
matter of dropping the two files in with the right name and adding the slug to
the LOCAL_DEMOS list in index.html and the DEMOS list in sw.js.

The service worker pre-caches all 150 on install, one at a time so a single
missing file cannot take the whole offline app down with it. They work in a
basement gym with no signal.
