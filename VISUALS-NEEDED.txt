HOW THEY GO IN
==============

Send me the photos and I do the rest. For the record, each one is two
files in demos/ named exactly as listed above, plus its slug added to
LOCAL_DEMOS in index.html and to DEMOS in sw.js so the service worker
pre-caches it for the garage. A local photograph outranks every remote
source automatically, so nothing else has to change.
