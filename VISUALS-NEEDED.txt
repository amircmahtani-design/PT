HOW THEY GO IN
==============

Send the photos and I do the rest. For the record: two files in demos/
named exactly as above, the slug added to LOCAL_DEMOS in index.html and
to DEMOS in sw.js so the service worker pre-caches it for the garage. A
local photograph outranks every remote source automatically, so nothing
else has to change.
