HOW TO SHOOT THEM
--------------------------------------------------------------
One WebP per movement, filename exactly as given below, dropped into
/demos/. The app already looks for these names — nothing else to do.

  · Landscape, phone on a tripod or a shelf, whole body and the whole
    apparatus in frame. A reformer shot that crops the footbar is no
    use for showing where the feet go.
  · Shoot the position that shows what the movement IS — usually the
    working end of it, not the start. Footwork means feet on the bar
    with the carriage out; Chest Expansion means arms already back.
  · Same room, same light, same clothes across a batch. They sit next
    to each other on screen.
  · Until a photo exists the app shows NO image rather than a wrong one,
    so there is no harm in doing these in batches.
