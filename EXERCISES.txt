NOT IN THE APP, DELIBERATELY
============================

Leg Press and Hack Squat were anchors in your legs pool. You have no leg
press and no hack squat, so the app was building blocks around machines in
someone else's gym. Removed, along with Leg Curl and Leg Extension.
Hamstrings are Romanian deadlifts, hip thrusts, Nordic curls and single-leg
work; quads are squats, split squats and lunges.

Barbell Row is IN, unrestricted, as an anchor in Pull and Upper. No cap,
no injury attached to it — you row 45kg fine. Your wrist is the only
injury the app programs around.
