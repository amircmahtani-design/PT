TWO FORMATS, BOTH WORKING
=========================

The first batch was pairs — demos/<slug>-1.jpg and -2.jpg — and the tile
cross-fades between the start and the finish. The second batch is one
WebP per movement with both positions already inside the frame, so those
tiles show a single still and size themselves to the image instead of
being letterboxed into a fixed box.

    76 movements as a JPG pair
    134 movements as a single WebP

IF ONE EVER LOOKS WRONG
=======================

Tell me the movement. A replacement is two files in demos/ named for its
slug, or one WebP named for the slug, plus the slug added to LOCAL_DEMOS
or LOCAL_WEBP in index.html and the matching list in sw.js so the
service worker pre-caches it. Nothing else has to change — a local
photograph outranks every remote source automatically.
