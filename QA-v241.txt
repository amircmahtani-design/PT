## Checked, not defects
- `--dim` on gradient-filled buttons: dark text on bright orange/green reads fine; my first checker ignored gradients and cried wolf.
- `SUMMARY "FIX AN ENTRY"` scrollWidth > clientWidth: `overflow:visible`, nothing is cut.
- `input[type=date]` showing 10/01/2026: native control follows the BROWSER locale, not the page. Day-first on his phone.
- `registerSW()` only registering over https: deliberate, and correct. Verified offline works by serving the app over real TLS.
- 37–38px segmented buttons and log-type chips: above the practical floor, wide, and raising them all would change the design for no real gain. Deliberate stop.

## Harness notes (things that fooled me, recorded so they don't again)
- Seeding without a reload leaves stale renders: the streak pill said 24 while the card said 0. Always seed → `save()` → reload.
- Date collisions in seed data produced a fake "Push Day 19d ago". Generated dates must not overlap.
- Contrast must composite rgba layers and skip gradient-backed text, or it reports the whole app as failing.
- Tap-target checks must read BOTH `::before` and `::after` for an absolutely-positioned reach box.
- Image checks must wait for `img.complete` before trusting `naturalWidth===0`.
